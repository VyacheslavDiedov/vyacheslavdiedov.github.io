Our product: Ads Tool

Ads Tool is our internal software that connects to advertising platforms through their official APIs, including the TikTok Marketing API. It is used only for advertising accounts we manage.

  1. Reporting. Collects campaign, ad group and ad level metrics (spend, impressions, CTR, CPM, conversions) into a single dashboard.
  2. Monitoring. Flags campaigns whose cost per result exceeds target thresholds.
  3. Management. Updates campaign statuses and budgets based on performance rules approved by our team.
  4. Security. Access is granted by the account owner through official OAuth authorization and can be revoked at any time.
