Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

Slavik Media ("we", "us") is a performance marketing studio. This policy explains how our internal software, Ads Tool, handles data obtained from advertising platforms, including the TikTok Marketing API.

2. Data we access

With the explicit authorization of the advertising account owner, Ads Tool accesses:

  • advertiser account information (account ID, name, currency, time zone);
  • campaign, ad group and ad settings;
  • aggregated performance metrics (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions).

We do not access or store personal data of TikTok users.

3. How we use data

Data is used only to report on and optimize advertising campaigns in accounts we manage. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and reports are stored securely and are accessible only to authorized team members. Data is kept only as long as needed for reporting and is deleted on request.

5. Revoking access

The account owner can revoke Ads Tool's access at any time in TikTok Business Center or TikTok Ads Manager. After revocation we no longer access the account.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: slavik@dim-link.com.
